DUNGEON ESCAPE -- EXPANDED EDITION  (Java 11+)
================================================

MAC:   Double-click RUN_MAC.command, or open Terminal inside this folder:
       java -jar DungeonEscape_Playable.jar

WIN:   Double-click RUN_WINDOWS.bat, or run:
       java -jar DungeonEscape_Playable.jar

LINUX: sh run_linux.sh

NEW CAMPAIGN: Choose Easy, Normal or Hard. Explore 3 acts, 15 rooms.
CLASSIC: Play the repaired original 4-room prototype.

WASD / ARROWS  Move           SPACE  Melee attack
E              Interact       1-8    Use/equip items
Q              Whirlwind      F      Firebolt
H              Heal           I      Inventory
M              Campaign map   X      Drop a bag item
P              Pause          R      Custom Rule Editor
K              Quick save     ESC    Menu

GOAL: In each act collect the north-room Sigil, gear up in the southern
room, fight the eastern Boss. After act III, reach the golden portal.

For details see README.md and USER_GUIDE.md.
For rebuilding: bash BUILD_JAR.sh
For tests:      bash RUN_TESTS.sh
